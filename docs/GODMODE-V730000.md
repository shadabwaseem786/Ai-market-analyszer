# V730000 — Microstructure & Order-Flow Intelligence

Adds bid/ask imbalance, trade aggressor classification, volume delta, cumulative delta, liquidity
walls, absorption and exhaustion heuristics, VWAP/AVWAP, volume profile, auction-state detection,
microstructure regimes and anomaly flags.

These are interfaces/heuristics that become live only when connected to suitable tick/order-book
data. They must not be interpreted as proof of hidden institutional activity or spoofing.
