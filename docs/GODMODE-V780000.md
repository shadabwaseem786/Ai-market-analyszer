# V780000 — Portfolio Risk & Position Intelligence

Adds position sizing, fractional Kelly, volatility targeting, portfolio Greeks aggregation,
parametric VaR/expected shortfall hooks, correlation clustering, stress scenarios, Monte Carlo,
risk-of-ruin flags, ATR-based stop/target framework, trade-quality grading and capital-preservation
gates.

Risk calculations are decision-support models. They are not guarantees, and live values depend on
reliable portfolio, volatility, options and market-data inputs.
