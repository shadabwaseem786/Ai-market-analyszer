# V770000 — Real-Time Event, News NLP & Shock Intelligence

Adds normalized news-event interfaces, event classification, baseline sentiment and entity extraction,
source reliability, duplicate suppression, event half-life decay, impact mapping, geopolitical and
macro shock classification, event-driven regime switching and Oracle fusion.

Live news requires a connected feed. The included sentiment component is intentionally a transparent
baseline rather than a claim of production-grade semantic understanding. Shock detection can gate
confidence and regime routing; it does not guarantee prediction of market reactions.
