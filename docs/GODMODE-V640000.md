# V640000 — Market Regime & Regime-Transition Intelligence 3.0

Adds multi-factor regime classification, empirical transition probabilities, early-warning alerts,
regime-specific model weights, signal thresholds, risk multipliers and historical regime scoring.

Regime classification is a decision-support layer. Thresholds and transition probabilities must be
calibrated on point-in-time data before being treated as predictive.
